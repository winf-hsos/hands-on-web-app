Anscombe argues against the view that calculations are exact and graphs
only rough. His evidence is four data sets of eleven points each, which have
the same means, standard deviations, the same correlation and the same
regression line and yet look completely different. Since this paper they
are called Anscombe's quartet.

## Why it fits here

The lesson [[why-visualise]] recomputes the statistics of the four data sets
in R and plots them side by side; the data are ready in R as `anscombe`. The
paper is short and easy to read without much statistics; at the journal it
is behind a paywall, but usually freely accessible through a university
library.
