The show “Geh aufs Ganze” was hosted by Jörg Draeger, and the idea is the
same as in the American original “Let's Make a Deal”: behind one of three
gates waits the main prize, the contestant picks one, and then it gets
exciting. The clip starts shortly before that moment. The show is in German,
but the game is easy to follow without understanding every word.

While watching, think about what you would have to write down about a round
to compare many rounds later: where the prize was, which gate was picked
first, which one was opened and what the contestant decided in the end. These
are exactly the facts that the [[monty-hall-game|three-door game]] records.
