When did artificial intelligence begin: in 1950 with Alan Turing, in 1956 at a workshop in Dartmouth, or in 2022 with ChatGPT? Instead of answering from a slide, we collect what you already know on a pinboard and hand it to an AI agent. The agent researches every milestone, reads the sources, checks a previous group's list for errors, and turns the result into an interactive timeline with a quiz.

The app that the agent built and published with one group is online: [A Brief History of AI](https://winf-hsos.github.io/a-brief-history-of-ai/).

What the agent does along the way is the programme of this module:

- How can a model read a photo of handwriting?
- How does a language model write a plan, code and text?
- Why does it use tools, and when are rules better than learning?
- Can we trust facts an AI has checked?
- What happens when an agent splits its work among sub-agents, or publishes on its own?
