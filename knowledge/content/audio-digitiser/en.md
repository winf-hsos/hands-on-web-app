**Goal:** Hear what the two cuts of digitising make of a sound. The sampling
rate sets how often per second the sound is measured, the bit depth to how
many loudness levels every measurement is rounded; the drawing shows the
staircase that comes out of it, and below it you see what the recording
costs.

**How to use:** Pick a sound at the top, set the sampling rate with the slider
and the bit depth below it, and compare the original with your digital
version using the buttons, best with headphones. You hear the cuts most
clearly at 2,000 Hz or at 2 bits.

