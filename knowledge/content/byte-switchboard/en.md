**Goal:** See how eight bits make a number. It is the sum of the place values
of the switches that are on, and the same byte appears in binary, decimal,
hexadecimal and as a character.

**How to use:** Click a switch to flip it, or drag across several. +1 and −1
count, and under decimal, hex and text you can type a value that the switches
follow. The byte starts at 65, the capital A.

```byte-switchboard
```
