The book accompanies an empirical study step by step: from the research topic
and the state of research through the study design, operationalisation and
sampling to data collection, preparation, analysis and the presentation of
results. Each step is explained for quantitative and qualitative research,
with many examples from psychology, sociology and communication science and
with notes on what a report has to contain.

## Why it fits here

Two chapters matter most for data analysis. Chapter 8, "Operationalisierung",
explains what a variable is and which four scales of measurement there are,
and shows for each which statements and calculations are allowed; it is also
where you read why ten degrees are not twice as warm as five and how to treat
rating scales. Section 11.4 describes how collected answers become a data
matrix, with a coding plan for every variable and clear codes for missing
values. You need both before you write the first line of R.

## Editions

The sixth, fully revised edition appeared in 2023 from Springer, in print
(ISBN 978-3-662-64761-5) and as an e-book. Earlier editions appeared under the
names Bortz and Döring. The book is written in German; university libraries
usually provide the e-book on SpringerLink.
