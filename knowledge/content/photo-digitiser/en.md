**Goal:** See what the two cuts of digitising make of a photo. The resolution
sets at how many points the picture is measured, the colour depth to how many
colours every point is rounded, and below the pictures you see what the result
costs in bytes and in transmission time.

**How to use:** Pick a picture at the top, set the resolution with the slider,
from 256 × 256 points down to a single one, and choose the colour depth below
it. Point at a spot of the digital picture to see its bits and the place in
the original it came from.

