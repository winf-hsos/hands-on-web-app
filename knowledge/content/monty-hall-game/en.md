**Goal:** Play the guessing game with three doors yourself and collect rounds
to find out whether you win more often when you stay with your door or when
you switch.

**How to use:** Pick a door. The host then opens another one with a goat
behind it, and you stay with your door or switch to the last closed one. Next to
the doors a statistic counts how often staying and switching win, and on
request the game shows all rounds as R code to copy or saves them as a table, as a CSV file or an RDS file with one row per round. The rounds stay stored in
this browser until you choose "Start over".
