The authors surveyed adults in Australia online and asked about their
actual consumption in a second wave. Using the theory of planned
behaviour, they test whether attitudes and beliefs about health,
environment and taste explain who intends to drink plant-based milk and who
drinks it. The paper is short, structured by introduction, methods, results
and discussion throughout, and open access under CC BY 4.0.

## Why it fits here

The lesson [[paper-structure]] uses it as an exercise: find the research
question at the end of the introduction and the answer at the beginning of
the discussion, and check whether results and discussion keep their jobs
apart. Topic and method are close to an analysis of the survey on milk and
chocolate.
