## What this is about

The R boxes on this platform are enough for learning. For analyses of your
own you need R on your computer and a place where you write scripts, run
them and look at data. Positron is such an environment, made by Posit, the
company behind RStudio and the tidyverse. It is free and handles R and
Python alike.

## Setting up

1. **Install R.** Positron does not bring R with it. The current version for
   Windows, macOS or Linux is available from CRAN at cran.r-project.org.
2. **Install Positron**, from the page linked below.
3. **Open a folder for your project.** Put data and scripts into one folder
   and open it in Positron with *File → Open Folder*. Then R finds every file
   there without a long path.
4. **Create a script** ending in `.R`, load the packages and get going. With
   **Ctrl+Enter** (**Cmd+Enter** on a Mac) you run the line the cursor is in.

## What you take with you from here

Everything you learned in the boxes works there just the same, including the
shortcut for the pipe. Only two things you have to do yourself: install and
load packages, as described in [[r-packages]], and read in your data, for
instance with `read_csv()`, as the page on the
[[milk-chocolate-survey|survey data set]] shows.
