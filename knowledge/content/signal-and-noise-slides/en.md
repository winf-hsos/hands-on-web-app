title: Signal and noise
summary: >-
  Why a link with the same hardware carries four symbols safely in one case
  and sixteen in another: readings scatter, and the size of the alphabet is
  decided by the ratio of the distance between the symbols to this scatter,
  which can be improved, but almost never for free.
objectives:
  intro: "Using a LiFi link made of an LED and a colour sensor, this deck shows you why the size of the alphabet depends on the scatter of the readings, and afterwards you can:"
  goals:
    - "explain why ten readings of the same colour give ten different numbers and why a single reading therefore says hardly anything"
    - "describe how averaging over longer windows calms the values and costs readings per second in return"
    - "argue why two symbols are only safely distinguishable when their clouds do not touch, and why the ratio `d/s` is what counts"
    - "calculate from the usable range and the width of one band how many symbols fit into a link"
    - "rank countermeasures against noise by their price in bits per symbol or symbols per second"
