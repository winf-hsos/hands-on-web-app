title: Same numbers, different pictures
summary: >-
  A point cloud travels from shape to shape, from a circle to a star to a
  heart, while the means, standard deviations and correlation stay the same
  to two decimals.
translation_of: de
based_on: 2026-11-16
---

**Goal:** See that the same summary statistics can belong to very different
data, and that only the chart shows what shape the data really have.

**How to use it:** Choose a shape at the top or go from one to the next with
"Next shape" and "Back"; "Play all" shows them one after another. On the
right are the statistics of the points on show. "Hide the points" leaves only
the numbers, so that you can first guess what the data look like before you
show them.
