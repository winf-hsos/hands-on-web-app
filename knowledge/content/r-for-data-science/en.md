## What it is about

The book shows how to analyse data with R, from importing it through tidying
and transforming it to the chart and the finished report. It starts with a
quick run through a whole analysis and then goes deeper into each step in a
part of its own: visualise, transform, import, program and communicate. The
tools come from the tidyverse, to which dplyr also belongs.

## Why it fits here

The lessons on dplyr think the same way: [[tidy-data|tidy data]] goes back
to Hadley Wickham, and the book explains the verbs from
[[data-transformation-basics]] in detail and with many more examples. If you
want more R after the path [[getting-started-with-r]], this is the next step.

## Editions

The second edition came out in 2023 from O'Reilly and largely reworks the
first; modelling has been dropped, and the authors point to tidymodels
instead. Online the book is free to read, under a Creative Commons licence
that allows sharing it unchanged and for non-commercial purposes.
