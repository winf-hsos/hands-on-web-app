LM Studio Bionic is an AI agent like OpenCode or Claude Code: you give it a
task, and it works on it on its own, reading and writing files, searching a
codebase, running commands and checking the result, until the task is done.
How such an agent works is explained in [[ai-agents]]. Bionic is made by the
company behind [[lm-studio]] and is a separate program; LM Studio stays the
place for chatting with a model and changing its settings.

Bionic works in **projects**: a project for documents creates and edits
reports, slides and tables, and a project for code works in a folder you
choose, with Git and the command line. Within a project, each task gets a
session of its own. Like other agents, Bionic can use skills in the
`SKILL.md` format.

In each session you choose where the model runs. A **local** model runs on
your own computer, is free and keeps your data on it, but must fit into its
memory. A **cloud** model runs on the maker's servers in the USA and needs an
account with a paid plan or credits.
