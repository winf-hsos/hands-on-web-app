Before the first lesson begins, you need your tools. The pages here are
meant for looking things up: you do not have to read them in one go, but come
back as soon as you need one of them.

What the device on your table is made of and what you may build around it is
on the page about the device.

![The LiFi device](item:lifi-device)

Before you write your first line of code, five programs have to be on your
laptop, the first of which you install by hand while your AI assistant takes
care of the rest.

![Required software](item:lifi-software)

Your programs talk to the LED and the sensor through the course module
`lifi_hardware`, which takes the technology off your hands but deliberately
leaves the measuring visible.

![The course module lifi_hardware](item:lifi-hardware)

![Looking up lifi_hardware](item:lifi-hardware-reference)

When nothing arrives on the link, look at the cockpit first, because it shows
live what your LED sends and what your sensor measures.

![The team cockpit](item:lifi-cockpit)

Which contract every challenge fixes and how it is accepted at the test table
is explained on the last page.

![Challenges in the LiFi project](item:lifi-challenges)
