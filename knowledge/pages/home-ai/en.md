This is where items on artificial intelligence are being written. They start
with what everyone knows, language models and agents, and from there go down
step by step: how a language model represents text, how it is trained and how
a machine learns from data at all. Then they climb back up to applications
and consequences. Every item stands on its own but points to what you should
know first and to what builds on it. Your browser remembers what you have
done, and it only leaves your computer if you take it along yourself.
