Here you will find computer science content that you can read, try out and
build yourself. Every item stands on its own but points to what you should
know beforehand and to what builds on it. You can follow a learning path,
look around on your own, or open the collection for your course with the code
from your lecture. Your browser remembers what you have already done, and that
record leaves your computer only if you take it along yourself.
