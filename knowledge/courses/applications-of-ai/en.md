This module accompanies the seminar. It starts with what everyone knows,
language models and the agents built on them, and works its way down from
there: how a language model represents text, how it is trained, and how a
machine learns from data at all, shown on much simpler cases. From there it
climbs back up to applications in different domains and to their
consequences. Every session begins with an experiment that you can repeat at
home, and all items are available from the start.
