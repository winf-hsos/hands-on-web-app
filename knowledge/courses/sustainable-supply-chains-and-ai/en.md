In the module Sustainable Transformation of the master's program Land Use
Transformation, lecturers from different fields present strategies for
sustainable land use in a lecture series. This session belongs to the topic
"Sustainable supply chains and artificial intelligence" and asks what an AI
agent can contribute to a supply chain in which less food is lost.

At the end of such a supply chain there is often a small shop in which,
every evening, someone decides what will keep until tomorrow and what is
marked down, given away or thrown out. In the session an AI agent takes
over, and in the game [[shelf-life]] you experience for one week how it goes
about it, while you shop there as shared flats. The
[[shelf-life-slides|introduction]] explains before the game how an agent
works and how an evening runs.

After the game, the slides [[inside-the-agent-slides]] look inside the
agent: how the language model in it writes text and is trained, what an
agent needs beyond that, and why you should not leave it everything it can
read.
