At the end of a food supply chain there is often a small shop in which,
every evening, someone decides what will keep until tomorrow and what is
marked down, given away or thrown out. In this module an AI agent takes
over, and in the game [[shelf-life]] you experience for one week how it goes
about it, while you shop there as shared flats. The
[[shelf-life-slides|introduction]] explains before the game how an agent
works and how an evening runs.

After the game, the slides [[inside-the-agent-slides]] look inside the
agent: how the language model in it writes text and is trained, what an
agent needs beyond that, and why you should not leave it everything it can
read.
