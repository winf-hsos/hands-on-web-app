This collection accompanies the part on exploratory data analysis in the
master's module Empirical Research. A self-assessment shows you at the start
where you stand. [[exploratory-data-analysis-slides|The slides]] then use the
survey on milk and chocolate to show what exploring achieves and which type
of question it can answer; after that one section introduces R, the next data
transformation with dplyr; further sections on describing, visualising
and checking data are being written right now and will also be available
from the start.
