This collection accompanies Nicolas Meseth's part of the bachelor's module
Practice of Market and Social Research. The module ends with a written paper
and a talk, and both are created here with Quarto from a single source in
which analysis, text and figures live together. What you need on your laptop
for it you set up under "Equipment", including the AI agent
[[opencode-course-assistant]], which knows the course folder.

Before the paper, the section "Getting started with data analysis in R" goes
the whole way from the file to the figure once, on slides at a glance. How
confident you already are afterwards is shown by the
[[survey-self-test|self-test]]: it checks your answers right on the page and,
for every task that is not right yet, names the chapter of the exercise where
you can catch up. The five chapters work at your own pace with the whole
survey, from a first look at the data set to the answer to a question of your
own.

The section after it lays the foundation for the paper: how a Quarto document
is built, what makes a good research question and how it fits data that have
already been collected, and how a scientific paper is structured. The
sections on AI agents in empirical research and on presenting the results
are added before the respective sessions.
